In phpMyAdmin, check appointments table:
```sql
SELECT 
    a.appointment_id,
    u.email,
    CONCAT(u.first_name, ' ', u.last_name) as resident_name,
    s.service_name,
    a.appointment_date,
    a.appointment_time,
    a.status
FROM appointments a
JOIN users u ON a.user_id = u.user_id
JOIN services s ON a.service_id = s.service_id
ORDER BY a.appointment_date;
```
